Everything in one place

Local when you want privacy. Cloud when you want power.

Switch between a model on your Mac and a model in the cloud in the same chat. Your history, files and projects stay on your machine.

Private, on-device models

Run GGUF models with llama.cpp or MLX on Apple Silicon. No account, no limits, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Picked for your Mac

Synapse reads your chip and memory and picks the best model and quantization that will actually run well.

Free cloud models

Sign in to Google AI Studio, Groq or OpenRouter, paste a free key, and use their models. Keys are stored in your Keychain.

Projects, files and instructions

Group chats into projects with their own folder and instructions. Models can read your documents, write new files and show you the results right in the chat.

See your limits

Live rate limits from Groq and request counts for every provider, so you know how much free usage is left.

FAQ

Questions

Is Synapse free?

Yes. Synapse is free and open source under the MIT license. Local models cost nothing to run. Cloud providers like Google AI Studio and Groq have free tiers; Synapse shows how much you've used.

Which Macs are supported?

Macs with Apple Silicon running macOS 14 or later. 8 GB of memory runs small models well; 16 GB or more opens up larger, smarter ones.

Does my data leave my Mac?

With local models, no: everything runs on your Mac. With cloud models, your messages go to the provider you chose. Chats, projects and files are always stored locally.

Can the AI control my computer?

Only if you turn on Computer Access or Browser for a chat. Commands, AppleScript and form submissions ask for your approval, and the browser never types passwords.

Where do the models come from?

Local models download straight from Hugging Face. You can also search the whole Hugging Face catalog for any GGUF or MLX model.
